# Contributing to ReviewRamble

Thank you for helping discover useful AI tools, models, MCP servers, and open-source repositories.

## Suggest a listing

**Preferred:** [Submit a tool to ReviewRamble](https://www.reviewramble.com/submit-tool).

You can also [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/swaroski/reviewramble/issues/new/choose). Include:

- Project name and official website or repository
- Category (AI tool, model, MCP server, or GitHub repository)
- What it does, who it is for, and its distinguishing features
- Pricing or license, supported platforms, and important limitations
- Documentation, source code, and evidence for factual claims
- Your relationship to the project (maintainer, employee, user, etc.)

Submissions do not guarantee inclusion, a review, or a particular rating. Maintainer-submitted projects are welcome; disclose the affiliation. Avoid affiliate tracking parameters and unverifiable superlatives.

## Listing standards

We favor useful, accessible, actively maintained projects with clear documentation and credible sources. Each entry should be accurate, concise, correctly categorized, and link to an official primary source. Security, license, platform, and pricing limitations should be mentioned where they matter.

A listing may be **unrated** if it has been accepted from a community suggestion but has not received an independent ReviewRamble review. Ratings and review links are published only when a corresponding review exists; inclusion does not mean an endorsement or security audit.

## Corrections and updates

For broken links, outdated claims, or missing caveats, [open an issue](https://github.com/swaroski/reviewramble/issues/new/choose) and cite an official source. Pull requests to improve factual accuracy, spelling, categorization, or accessibility are welcome.

When editing the README:
1. Keep its categories, headings, and table of contents consistent.
2. Use concise, factual descriptions; avoid marketing copy.
3. Prefer official project URLs, and remove referral or tracking parameters.
4. Update section totals if you add or remove a listing.
5. Verify any rating is supported by a published ReviewRamble review.

## Independence and attribution

Community suggestions are not paid placements. Please disclose conflicts of interest. List text is shared under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) with attribution to ReviewRamble.
